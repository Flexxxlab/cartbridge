FAQ: Walmart cart API and grocery cart links

What is a Walmart cart API?

An API that takes a shopping list and returns a Walmart cart. CartBridge matches each ingredient to a Walmart product and returns an add-to-cart link, so your app can send a shopper to a filled cart.

How do I add Walmart add to cart to a Flutter app?

Run flutter pub add cart_bridge, then call CartBridge(apiKey: ...).openCart(context, items: ...). See Getting started.

Do I need my own server?

No. CartBridge holds the part that signs requests with your Walmart key and talks to Walmart.

Do I need a Walmart account?

Yes. You bring your own Walmart Affiliate API credentials from walmart.io. Walmart applies its own terms and quotas to them.

What is a grocery cart link?

A URL such as https://affil.walmart.com/cart/addToCart?items=ITEMID|QTY,.... Opening it adds the items to the shopper's Walmart cart. They sign in and check out on Walmart.

Is my Walmart private key safe?

It is stored encrypted, never shown again after you save it, and never sent to your app. Your app holds a CartBridge key that you can revoke at any time without touching your Walmart key.
