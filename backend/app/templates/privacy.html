Privacy Policy

Last updated 11 November 2026. {{ company_name }} runs CartBridge. This page says what we collect and why.

What we collect

  • Account data: username, email address and a salted hash of your password (we cannot read your password).
  • API keys: only a hash and a short prefix. The full key is shown to you once.
  • Walmart credentials you add: consumer ID, key version, optional publisher ID and your private key, stored encrypted.
  • Usage records: the time of each cart request and monthly counts per account, to enforce limits. We do not store the contents of your ingredient lists.
  • Network data: your IP address on sign-up, login and verification requests, kept up to 24 hours to prevent abuse. Our web server and host may keep ordinary access logs.
  • Cookie: one session cookie (cb_session) to keep you logged in. We use no advertising or analytics cookies.
  • Website measurement: when you visit the CartBridge website we count anonymous visits and any clicks on store buttons, along with the campaign link you arrived from, if any. This runs through our own service at go.flexxxlab.com and uses a random visitor identifier stored only in your browser's session storage. It is not linked to any account, and we do not collect IP addresses, names, email addresses or other personal data through it. Visitor identifiers expire after 30 days.

How we use it

To run your account, verify your email, authenticate API calls, enforce rate limits, keep the service secure and answer your messages. We do not sell your data.

Who else sees it

Your ingredient names, and your Walmart credentials, are sent to Walmart to search products and sign requests. Walmart's own privacy policy applies to that. Our hosting provider processes data on our behalf. We disclose data if the law requires it.

How long we keep it

Account data until you ask us to delete the account. Request timestamps for about a day, monthly counts while the account exists, network attempt records up to 24 hours.

Your choices

You can remove your Walmart credentials and revoke API keys in the dashboard at any time. To see, correct or delete your data, email {{ support_email }} from the address on your account.

Security

Passwords are hashed with scrypt, API keys are stored as hashes, Walmart private keys are encrypted at rest, and forms are CSRF protected. No system is perfectly secure; report problems as described in SECURITY.md.

Changes

We will update the date above when this policy changes. Contact: {{ support_email }}.
